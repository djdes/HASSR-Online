# Shared WeSetup copy and imagery

Shared pages serve medical centres, food service, manufacturing and other supported organisations. A profession or a kitchen is not the default identity of a WeSetup customer.

- Shared guidance uses «сотрудник», «ответственный сотрудник», «руководитель», «организация», «точка», «помещение» and «оборудование». Use a specific profession only when the task or industry requires it.
- Use the existing WeSetup wordmark and notebook icon for the product, initials for people and the organisation’s own identity for its workspace. Shared illustrations should show the action, log, equipment or workspace rather than a person dressed for one profession. Use the existing Lucide icon system.
- Public staff demonstrations use a fictional organisation and neutral positions such as manager, administrator, specialist and cleaner. Capture actual components with synthetic data. Never fabricate a product screenshot or alter customer data for a marketing capture.
- Food-specific pages, fryer scenes, food hygiene posters and food journal examples can show cooks and kitchens. Identify the industry when such an example appears on a shared page. A kitchen video is an example of food service, not a promise about every industry.
- Actual job titles, role labels and permission preset names must describe what the product actually assigns. Do not rename a food-specific assignment preset to imply that it automatically supports medical positions. Explain its scope and allow manual assignment.
- Keep shared copy aligned in Russian, English and Uzbek. Preserve ICU parameters, plural branches, rich-text links and the generated translation contract. Do not broaden legal claims or make healthcare compliance promises through an editorial change.
