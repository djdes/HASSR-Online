## Full description

```
Setup Helper prints kitchen and food-production labels on a Bluetooth label printer — fast, with big buttons and without internet. It is part of WeSetup, electronic food-safety (HACCP) logs for restaurants and canteens.

WHAT YOU CAN PRINT
• Product label: name, prepared at, use-by date, responsible person
• Semi-finished product and ready dish
• Defrosting and freezing
• Daily sample
• Incoming goods
• Allergens
• Opened package
• Rejected — do not use
• Free text

HOW IT WORKS
1. Sign in with your venue's WeSetup account.
2. "Add equipment" finds the printer nearby and connects it.
3. Pick a template, fill in the fields — use-by dates are calculated automatically — and tap "Print".

MADE FOR THE KITCHEN
• Large template buttons with arrows to scroll.
• Preview shows exactly the label the printer will produce.
• Print history: repeat yesterday's label in one tap, or edit and print again.
• Works offline: printing goes straight to the printer.

PRINTERS
Tested with AIYIN IP-802BT (58×40 mm labels, TSPL commands). Other Bluetooth TSPL label printers also work. Label size, gap, density and rotation are adjustable.

The app interface is in Russian. A WeSetup account (wesetup.ru) is required. Questions: support@wesetup.ru.
```
