# Problems found by the independent review of series E (2026-11-21)

The reviewer read commits 674528a5d and 13a61a8c1. There were no critical findings: the money path and the manager role checks hold.

| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
|---|----------|---------|------------|
| 1 | Medium | A network manager can move every balance into one organization (`/api/balance/transfer`, granted by Р5) and then pay the tariff from that organization's balance through `/order` (`usePoints`), or turn on auto-renew. | **Decision: keep, document.** Paying with an organization's balance through `/order` and auto-renew is allowed to any user with full access in that organization (`hasFullWorkspaceAccess`). That rule predates series E and series E does not change it. A network manager can only be appointed from people who already have full access in the account's organizations (`eligiblePeople` / `hasFullAccessInAccount`). So they could already pay from those organizations before the appointment. The appointment adds transfers between the account's own organizations; money never leaves the account and every transfer is logged with the manager's name. "Only the owner pays" in Р5/AC5 means the new account-level payment: «Собрать с организаций аккаунта и оплатить» (`account-pay`, owner-only, checked by `pay-from-account-check`), plus appointing managers and deciding foreign requests. Restricting `/order` for every non-owner would take an existing right away from all organization managers, which the owner did not ask for. Raised with the owner in the series report. |
| 2 | Low | Undo by id (transfers, exchange history) checked the target's account, not the session's account. A manager of X who owns Y could undo X's items while working in Y. | Fixed: the routes pass the session's account and the undo functions refuse a target from another account; checked in `manager-check`. |
| 3 | Low | Batched product creation reads taken names once, then flushes minutes later. A product added by hand in that window could be duplicated. | Fixed: the chunk's names are re-read right before each insert. |
| 4 | Low | Weekly digest dedupe had a check-then-mark race between overlapping cron runs, so email could go out twice. | Fixed: the claim is taken first, atomically (advisory transaction lock + mark), and released if nothing was delivered. |
| 5 | Low | The period summary loaded every legacy journal entry of 30 days per organization. | Fixed: counts are aggregated in SQL (`GROUP BY` template and Moscow day) and give the same numbers. |
| 6 | Low | The series E scenarios lived only in `.agent/.../raw`, so CI never ran them. The PR `paths` filter missed the new code. AC3 memory had a thin margin. | Fixed: `manager-check`, `pay-from-account-check`, `cleanup-check` and `period-digest-check` moved to `scripts/e2e/` with wrappers in `raw/` and were added to `run-all.sh`. `paths` were extended. The memory note was added to `raw/load-exchange.log`. |
| 7 | UX | On the site, «Перейти» was shown for organizations a manager is not a member of, and switching failed. | Fixed: the site uses the same `canOpen` rule as the mini app. |

Results after the fixes are recorded in `evidence.md` / `evidence.json`.
